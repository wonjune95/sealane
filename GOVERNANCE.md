## Decision making

Decisions are discussed in public, in issues and pull requests, and made by consensus of the maintainers. Maintainers aim to respond within 7 days on a best-effort basis.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contribute steadily for three months or more (code, reviews, issue triage) and get the approval of every existing maintainer. The change is a pull request that adds you to the table above.

## Stepping down

Open a pull request that removes you from the table.

## Code of conduct

Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). The maintainers enforce it.

## Changes to this document

By pull request, approved by the maintainers.
